PAYMENT INFORMATION

Refund Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This policy explains how refund requests for purchases made through StudyFlow are handled.

1. Payment Processing

Payments on StudyFlow are processed through Paystack. Refund requests are subject to payment verification and applicable payment provider procedures.

2. Eligible Refund Requests

You may contact us to request a review in situations such as:

  • You were charged more than once for the same purchase.
  • Your payment was successful but the purchased service was not activated.
  • A transaction was processed incorrectly.
  • A tutoring session was cancelled or not delivered as agreed.
  • Another circumstance where a refund is required by applicable law.

3. Digital Study Materials

If you experience a problem accessing purchased study materials, contact support first so we can investigate. Refund eligibility will depend on the circumstances, applicable law and whether the digital content has been supplied or accessed.

4. Premium Memberships

If your Premium membership payment succeeds but your membership is not activated, contact us for assistance. Other cancellation and refund requests will be reviewed according to the circumstances and applicable consumer protection requirements.

5. Tutoring Services

Refund requests involving tutoring sessions will be reviewed based on the payment record, session arrangements, cancellation circumstances and any applicable tutor terms.

6. How to Request a Refund

Please provide:

  • The email address associated with your StudyFlow account.
  • Your Paystack transaction reference.
  • The item or service purchased.
  • A description of the payment problem.

Submit your request through our Contact Us section. Never send your password, card PIN or full card details.

7. Review and Processing

We will review each request and communicate the outcome. Approved refunds will be processed through the appropriate payment provider procedure. Processing times may depend on the payment provider and financial institution.

8. Legal Rights

Nothing in this policy removes any refund or consumer rights that cannot legally be excluded under applicable law.

TUTOR GUIDELINES

Tutor Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This policy explains the responsibilities and expectations of tutors using StudyFlow.

1. Tutor Registration

  • Tutors must provide accurate profile information.
  • Subject descriptions and qualifications must be truthful.
  • Tutors must provide valid contact and availability information.
  • StudyFlow may review tutor profiles before or after publication.

2. Professional Conduct

Tutors are expected to:

  • Treat students respectfully and professionally.
  • Provide lessons in the subjects listed on their profiles.
  • Respect agreed lesson times and arrangements.
  • Communicate changes or cancellations promptly.
  • Maintain appropriate boundaries with students.

3. Tutor Pricing

Tutors must display accurate session prices in Nigerian naira where applicable. Any agreed changes to pricing should be communicated clearly before a student makes payment.

4. Payments and Commission

Tutoring payments are processed through the payment arrangements provided by StudyFlow. Applicable platform commissions, tutor earnings and payout conditions must be communicated clearly.

Tutors should not misrepresent payment status or request unauthorized payments using StudyFlow's name.

5. Student Safety

  • Tutors must not harass, threaten or exploit students.
  • Personal information shared during tutoring must be handled responsibly.
  • Any safeguarding concerns should be reported promptly.
  • Communication with minors must follow applicable safeguarding requirements.

6. Learning Materials

Tutors must only upload materials they own or are authorized to distribute. Copyright infringement and unauthorized sharing are not permitted.

7. Cancellations and Disputes

Tutors should communicate cancellations promptly and cooperate with reasonable investigations into student complaints, missed sessions and payment disputes.

8. Prohibited Conduct

Fraud, fake qualifications, misleading profiles, harassment, academic misconduct and attempts to manipulate payments are prohibited.

9. Policy Violations

StudyFlow may investigate reported violations and restrict or suspend tutor accounts where necessary, subject to applicable law and fair review procedures.

10. Questions

For tutor-related questions, visit our Contact Us section.
