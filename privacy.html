Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026

StudyFlow respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you access or use the StudyFlow website and its services.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use StudyFlow, we may collect:

  • Account information, such as your email address and account details.
  • Profile information supplied by students and tutors.
  • Assignments, study activities, quiz attempts, and results.
  • Tutoring requests, session information, and tutor profiles.
  • Messages exchanged between students and tutors.
  • Payment references, transaction statuses, and purchase records.
  • Technical information, such as browser type, device information, and website usage data.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Create and manage student and tutor accounts.
  • Provide assignments, quizzes, study materials, and tutoring services.
  • Enable communication between students and tutors.
  • Process purchases and maintain payment records.
  • Calculate and manage tutor earnings and payouts.
  • Improve website performance, security, and user experience.
  • Respond to support requests and protect the platform against misuse.

3. Account Information and Storage

StudyFlow uses Supabase services for account authentication, database storage, and certain platform features. Information associated with your account may be stored and processed through these services.

You should keep your login credentials private and contact us if you believe someone has accessed your account without permission.

4. Payments and Financial Information

StudyFlow uses Paystack to facilitate payments. Payment processing is subject to Paystack's own privacy and security practices.

StudyFlow may receive and store transaction references, payment statuses, purchase details, and related records needed to manage purchases and tutor payments.

Do not send passwords, card security codes, or other sensitive payment credentials through StudyFlow messages or support channels.

5. Student and Tutor Messages

StudyFlow provides messaging features that allow students and tutors to communicate about tutoring services. Messages may be stored so that conversations can be displayed and delivered to the relevant participants.

Only share information that is necessary for your tutoring communication. Do not use messaging to share passwords or sensitive financial information.

6. Cookies, Analytics, and Advertising

StudyFlow may use cookies and similar technologies to support website functionality, understand website usage, and improve services.

Google Analytics may be used to measure website traffic and understand how visitors interact with StudyFlow.

Google AdSense or other advertising services may display advertisements. These services may use cookies or similar technologies in accordance with their own privacy policies and applicable choices and settings.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking certain cookies may affect some website features.

7. Third-Party Services

StudyFlow relies on third-party services that may process information as necessary to provide their services. These may include:

  • Supabase for authentication and database services.
  • Paystack for payment processing.
  • Google Analytics for website analytics.
  • Google AdSense for advertising, where enabled.
  • OpenAI services for StudyFlow AI features, where used.

These providers have their own privacy policies and terms. Their handling of information is governed by their applicable policies.

8. Information Sharing

We do not sell personal information to advertisers.

Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate StudyFlow, with relevant students or tutors to provide requested services, or where disclosure is required by law or necessary to protect the rights and security of users and the platform.

9. Data Retention and Security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for account operation, service delivery, security, legal obligations, and legitimate business purposes.

We use available technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect information. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

10. Your Privacy Choices and Requests

You may contact StudyFlow to ask about personal information associated with your account, request corrections, or request account or data deletion.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law, for legitimate security purposes, or to maintain necessary transaction records.

11. Children's Privacy

StudyFlow is an educational platform. Users who are children should use the service with appropriate parent or guardian involvement where required by applicable law.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, practices, or legal requirements change. The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised effective date where appropriate.
